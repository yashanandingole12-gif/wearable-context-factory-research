Problem & Context Asymmetry

Modern vehicle plants integrate workers, cobots, fixed stamping machinery, and environmental sensors under Industry 5.0.

The Asymmetry Gap: Machines track production and equipment state, but have limited direct access to changing human context such as workload, fatigue indicators, posture, location, and safety state.
• Static Curtains: Rigid optical fences cause abrupt stops upon line-of-sight breach.
• Cumulative Strain Blindness: Repetitive posture overload leads to MSDs undetected by machinery.
• Uncoordinated Pacing: Cobots hold uniform speeds regardless of worker fatigue.
2

Core Research Questions

RQ1 • State Estimation: Can multimodal wearable signals provide useful worker-state indicators in real time?
RQ2 • Uncertainty Scoring: Can a confidence metric ($C_t$) prevent erratic robot motion during packet drop?
RQ3 • Safety Detection: Does sensor fusion detect stumbles and pinch hazards sooner than laser walls?
RQ4 • Cobot Adaptation: Can cobots dynamically derate speed and shift handoff height without line delays?
RQ5 • Network & Robustness: How do battery life, 15-40% packet drops, and privacy affect field adoption?

Proposed Hierarchical Architecture

1. WEARABLE SENSING LAYER Synthetic stream

IMU (wrist/spine), Chest PPG/ECG, Skin Temp, UWB Tag. Ultra-low power BLE 5.2.

↓ BLE / MQTT Stream Ingestion ↓
2. EDGE GATEWAY & DATA CLEANING Edge Gateway

Timestamped event records, packet-loss flags, device state, SQLite persistence and queryable history.

↓ Denoised Worker Telemetry ↓
3. CONTEXT & DECISION ENGINE Inference

State classification: Nominal, Fatigue Strain, Hazardous Reach.

Confidence: $C_t = \alpha(1-\text{loss}) + \beta(1-\sigma^2_{\text{IMU}}) + \gamma(\text{SNR})$
Weights: $\alpha=0.4, \beta=0.3, \gamma=0.3$. Smooth degradation under dropouts.
↓ Advisory Velocity Derate & Ergonomic Pacing ↓
4. HMI / HRI INTERACTION LAYER Safety Guard

Confidence-aware robot action: continue, reduce speed, safety stop, or machine-safe-stop.

⚠️ Research prototype only: wearable context does not replace safety-rated physical controls.

Core Research Contributions

1. Hierarchical Architecture: Multi-tiered separation of advisory human context from hardwired ISO 10218 safety relays.
2. Virtual Factory Twin: Complete 10-station automotive body, powertrain, and assembly discrete simulation.
3. Synthetic Sensor Generator: Synchronized IMU, PPG, and cobot telemetry with parametric noise injection.
4. Context-Confidence Metric ($C_t$): Continuous uncertainty quantification preventing erratic cobot behavior.
5. Baseline & Ablation Benchmark: Executed synthetic comparison of none, motion, physiology, glasses and full conditions.
